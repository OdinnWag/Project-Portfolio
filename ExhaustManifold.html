Project Objective

Design a 4-1 performance exhaust that utilizes exhaust scavenging to optimize high-rpm torque output. Notably, this design is biased towards higher peak horsepower, with a less broad torque curve than 4-2-1 variants.

Summary

As a member of the Terps Racing 2024 Powertrain Team, I set out to design an updated performance exhaust. An exhaust manifold with equal length headers, tuned to an ideal torque peak at 10k rpm could theoretically result in significant performance gains. Previous designs had been technically ambitious, but ran into manufacturing issues that significantly hampered any realized torque gains. Due to these previous DFM issues, I could only use 30, 45, and 90 degree titanium bend cuts for this design, which posed significant issues for fitting the exhaust headers within the vehicle.
